November 2026 — Initial release

Nov 16, 2026

  • Initial release with seven catalogs: Harnesses 33, Lanyards 20, SRLs 32, Anchors 29, Connectors 19, Lifelines 13, Rescue & descent 10 — 156 rows in all.
  • Added Basics & standards: OSHA 1926.502 / 1910.140 fall arrest limits, trigger heights, ANSI/ASSP Z359 and CSA Z259 overview, fall clearance, swing falls, rescue planning, and inspection — in plain language with links to the official sources.
  • Added the Compare tool: tick up to four rows from any catalog and view every field, source and link side by side.
  • Sourcing: specs come from official manufacturer pages, spec sheets and instruction manuals, plus public retailer listings for the exact part number, checked Nov 16, 2026. Every row links its source; notes are in our own words.
  • Prices: 129 rows have a live retailer price with a source link and check date; 27 show an editorial band. Price = lowest in-stock price for a new unit of that exact part number seen on a public US retailer or manufacturer-store listing (seller named and linked), checked on the date shown. No affiliate links. If no live price was found, a $ band is shown as an editorial estimate.
  • Editorial price bands (shown only when no live price was seen): $ under $100 · $$ $100–$250 · $$$ $250–$600 · $$$$ $600–$1,500 · $$$$$ over $1,500.
