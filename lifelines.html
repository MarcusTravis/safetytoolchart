Lifelines

Portable horizontal lifeline kits that let workers move along a span, plus rope grabs and vertical lifelines. Horizontal lifelines have to be designed and installed under a qualified person.

Data last checked: Nov 25, 2026 · What changed

Safety note. Reference only — not training and not a substitute for the manufacturer’s instructions, a competent person, or OSHA/ANSI requirements. Inspect gear before each use, remove from service any equipment that has arrested a fall, and verify specs with the manufacturer.

A horizontal lifeline lets a worker move along a span while staying connected. OSHA’s 1926.502(d) says horizontal lifelines are designed, installed and used under a qualified person with a safety factor of at least two, and that each worker on a vertical lifeline needs their own line. Lanyards and vertical lifelines need a minimum breaking strength of 5,000 lb. Read the maker’s instructions before use, and see the OSHA limits on Basics.
