Rescue & descent

Gear for getting a fallen worker down or back up quickly: controlled-descent devices, pre-rigged rescue kits, retrieval systems, and straps that relieve leg pressure while waiting for rescue.

Data last checked: Nov 16, 2026 · What changed

Safety note. Reference only — not training and not a substitute for the manufacturer’s instructions, a competent person, or OSHA/ANSI requirements. Inspect gear before each use, remove from service any equipment that has arrested a fall, and verify specs with the manufacturer.

Arresting a fall is only half the job: OSHA’s 1926.502(d)(20) requires prompt rescue or the ability for workers to rescue themselves. A worker hanging in a harness can develop suspension intolerance, so the plan, gear and trained people need to be in place before work starts. This chart lists descent and rescue devices, retrieval systems and suspension relief straps. Rescued workers should be medically evaluated; see Suspension and rescue planning.
