Connectors

Self-locking snap hooks, rebar hooks and carabiners. Gate strength is the number that stops a hook being forced open sideways; the table shows it where the maker publishes it.

Data last checked: Nov 16, 2026 · What changed

Safety note. Reference only — not training and not a substitute for the manufacturer’s instructions, a competent person, or OSHA/ANSI requirements. Inspect gear before each use, remove from service any equipment that has arrested a fall, and verify specs with the manufacturer.

Connectors link the harness, lanyard or SRL, and anchor together. Under Z359.12 snap hooks and carabiners must be self-closing and self-locking with gates rated to 3,600 lbf, which is the Gate lbf column here. OSHA’s 1926.502(d) sets D-ring and snap hook minimum tensile strength at 5,000 lb, proof-tested to 3,600 lb, and allows only locking snap hooks; 1910.140 adds the automatic-locking requirement.
